## What is PYTHIA?

Every day, people solve problems, discover better ways of working, and build useful tools. But too often, that knowledge stays with one person, one team, or one conversation.

**PYTHIA brings that collective experience together.**

It helps colleagues:

- find an existing answer or reusable solution;
- connect with people who have relevant experience;
- share expertise, project knowledge, and useful assets;
- identify recurring problems worth solving;
- recognise the people who contribute to collective knowledge.

PYTHIA is not just a chatbot. It is a shared knowledge system that connects questions, people, experience, and solutions.

---

## How it works

### 1. Ask

Describe a problem in your own words through **Seek Wisdom**.

PYTHIA looks for:

- relevant solutions already available in the catalogue;
- colleagues whose expertise and experience match the request;
- other potential matches that may still be able to help.

If no suitable answer exists, PYTHIA can suggest what kind of solution, playbook, or agent may be worth creating.

### 2. Find

PYTHIA combines exact matches with semantic relevance.

This means it can recognise both:

- specific terms such as Salesforce, Jira, SAP, or Databricks;
- similar meanings expressed in different words.

The most relevant candidates are reviewed before PYTHIA returns its recommendation.

### 3. Share

Use **Share Wisdom** to describe something you know, have delivered, or have built.

PYTHIA can identify:

- areas of expertise;
- project experience;
- reusable assets such as agents, automations, tools, guides, and playbooks.

Nothing is saved immediately. You can review, edit, or remove everything PYTHIA extracts before sharing it.

### 4. Learn

Every request helps reveal what the organisation already knows — and what it is still missing.

The **Discoveries** area highlights:

- the most common requests;
- problems that already have solutions;
- questions that can be answered by colleagues;
- recurring gaps that may be worth solving;
- solutions that are actively needed or currently underused.

## Seek Wisdom

Bring PYTHIA a real problem:

> “Who has experience implementing Salesforce for a retail bank?”

> “Is there already a solution for turning customer calls into CRM notes?”

> “My calendar is full of client meetings. How can I make room to think?”

PYTHIA may return:

- a recommended solution;
- the experts best suited to help;
- additional potential matches;
- a proposal for something new that could be created.

Recommendations are grounded in the knowledge and profiles available to PYTHIA rather than generated from nothing.

---

## Share Wisdom

Knowledge becomes valuable when other people can find and reuse it.

You can share:

- an area of expertise;
- experience from a project;
- a repeatable way of working;
- an automation or internal tool;
- an agent, guide, or playbook;
- a lesson that could help someone facing the same problem.

PYTHIA turns the contribution into a structured draft, while you remain in control of what is saved.

---

## Discoveries

Discoveries turns individual questions into an organisation-wide view.

It helps answer questions such as:

- What problems are people asking about most often?
- Which requests already have a solution?
- Where can colleagues help even when no formal solution exists?
- Which gaps appear often enough to justify building something?
- Which available solutions are useful, and which are not being discovered?

This creates a feedback loop:

**Ask → Find → Share → Reuse → Discover what to build next**

---

## Collective recognition

PYTHIA recognises people who strengthen collective knowledge.

Colleagues earn laurels by sharing useful knowledge, helping others, and contributing solutions that are reused. The **Pantheon** makes those contributions visible and celebrates the people behind them.

The goal is not competition for its own sake. It is to make knowledge-sharing valuable, visible, and part of everyday work.

---

## Calendar Breather

PYTHIA can also help protect time for focused work and recovery.

Calendar Breather reviews the upcoming schedule, finds suitable gaps, and suggests short breaks during demanding workdays. Every proposed break remains under the user’s control and can be accepted or declined.

---

## Current status

PYTHIA is currently a **local working prototype**.

It is not publicly hosted and is not yet available as a production service. The current version is being used to validate the product experience, search quality, knowledge-sharing model, and integrations.

A hosted version may be introduced in the future.
